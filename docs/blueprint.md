# **App Name**: ASSERWA Portal

## Core Features:

- Member Registration & Onboarding: A streamlined UI for sanitation professionals to register, submit credentials, and join the association database.
- Sanitation Standards Compliance Tool: An AI-powered tool that uses reasoning to evaluate sanitation service reports and site maintenance plans against Rwandan national professional standards.
- Knowledge & Reports Repository: A secure storage and retrieval system for technical sanitation reports, environmental publications, and a download center for educational resources.
- Project Impact & Statistics Dashboard: A visual UI displaying real-time impact metrics such as communities reached, members trained, and facilities supported across Rwanda.
- Advocacy & News Management System: A platform to manage and publish blog updates, stakeholder engagement events, and advocacy initiatives between practitioners and government bodies.
- Partnership & Donation Gateway: A secure communication and inquiry module for development partners and donors to support sanitation initiatives via a dedicated donation section.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Green (#6cb166) representing environmental protection, hygiene, and sanitation excellence.
- Secondary color: Blue (#3b66b0) representing professional advocacy, trust, and public health.
- Background: Clean White (#FFFFFF) to emphasize hygiene and a modern NGO-grade feel.
- Headlines use 'Montserrat' for a bold, impactful, and professional presence.
- Body text uses 'Lato' for high readability in technical reports and community resource materials.
- A mix of technical sanitation symbols (septic maintenance, clean water drops) and community-centric icons (linked hands, public health).
- Mobile-responsive, structured grid-based layout inspired by modern international NGO and World Bank project sites.
- Subtle, professional fade-in transitions and progress bars to visualize growth in community reach statistics.
